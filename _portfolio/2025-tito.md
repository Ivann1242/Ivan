At Michigan’s LoGM, I worked with [Grant Barkley](https://lsa.umich.edu/math/people/postdoc-faculty/gbarkley.html), a postdoctoral assistant professor of mathematics, from September 2025 to April 2026. He advised our team on translation-invariant total orders.

We developed algorithms that compare orders through their inversion sets and compute their joins using weighted paths in a graph. We implemented them in [TITO_Explore](https://github.com/Ivann1242/TITO_Explore_package/tree/v0.1.2), a package that also normalizes and visualizes these orders.

[Computing Tools for Translation-Invariant Total Orders](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.11709), with Emma Yicheng Lou, Shaotian Sun, and Yuxuan Wu. Under review at FPSAC 2027.
